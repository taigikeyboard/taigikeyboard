# Taiwanese Phonetics Reference

Cross-system comparison of Taiwanese romanization and phonetic symbols, compiled from official MOE documents and linguistic references.

**Systems covered:**
- **TL** (Tâi-lô / 臺灣台語羅馬字拼音方案) — MOE official standard (2006, revised 2024)
- **TPS** (方音符號 / 臺灣方音符號) — bopomofo-based phonetic symbols for Taiwanese
- **POJ** (Pe̍h-ōe-jī / 白話字 / 教會羅馬字) — traditional missionary romanization
- **IPA** (International Phonetic Alphabet)

**Sources:**
- 教育部《臺灣台語羅馬字拼音方案使用手冊》(民國 113 年 8 月修訂版)
- 立開成《三種台語拼音書寫系統——臺羅、白話字、方音符號佮國際音標的對照》(2025 年 10 月修訂版)
- 《台語書寫標點符號 ài chim-chiok ê 代誌》(2025 年 8 月金花更新)

## 1. Syllable Structure

A Taiwanese syllable has three components:

```
聲 (shēng)          調 (diào)
┌──────┐    ┌───────────────────────┐
│聲母    │    │    韻母                │
│Initial│    │韻頭 + 韻腹 + 韻尾      │
│       │    │Glide  Nucleus  Coda   │
└──────┘    └───────────────────────┘
```

- **Glide (韻頭):** i, u
- **Nucleus (韻腹):** a, e, i, oo, o, u, ann, enn, inn, onn, unn, m, ng
- **Coda (韻尾):** i, u, m, n, ng, p, t, k, h

| Dental/Alveolar | Voiceless aspirated affricate | **tsh** | [tsʰ] | ㄘ | chh |
| Dental/Alveolar | Voiced affricate | **j** | [dz] | ㆡ | j |
| Dental/Alveolar | Voiceless fricative | **s** | [s] | ㄙ | s |
| Velar | Voiceless unaspirated stop | **k** | [k] | ㄍ | k |

| Velar | Voiced stop | **g** | [g] | ㆣ | g |
| Glottal | Voiceless fricative | **h** | [h] | ㄏ | h |
| (zero initial) | — | — | — | — | — |

### Notes on initials
- TL `ts`, `tsh`, `s`, `j` correspond to POJ `ch`, `chh`, `s`, `j` — these are the main differences between TL and POJ in initials
- TPS ㆠ is the voiced counterpart of ㄅ; ㆣ is the voiced counterpart of ㄍ; ㆡ is the voiced counterpart of ㄗ
- When [ts], [tsʰ], [s], [dz] combine with [i] (or [ɪ]), they become palatalized [tɕ], [tɕʰ], [ɕ], [dʑ]. In TPS, the symbols ㄐ (ts+i), ㄑ (tsh+i), ㄒ (s+i), ㆢ (j+i) are used before ㄧ
- Quanzhou dialect generally lacks the `j` initial; it merges with [l]

## 3. Finals (韻母)

### 3.1 Basic Vowels

| IPA | TL | TPS | POJ | Example |
|-----|----|----|-----|---------|
| [a] | a | ㄚ | a | 亞 a |
| [i] | i | ㄧ | i | 椅 í |
| [u] | u | ㄨ | u | 輸 su |
| [e] | e | ㄝ | e | 禮 lé |
| [ɔ] | oo | ㄛ | o͘ | 姑 koo |
| [o] | o | ㄜ | o | 蚵 ô |
| [ə] | o | ㄜ | o | — |

- TL `oo` = POJ `o͘` (with superscript dot). The IPA value is [ɔ].
- TL `o` = POJ `o`. The IPA value is [ə] or [o] depending on dialect. The actual pronunciation ranges across [o], [ʏ], [ɔ] dialectally.
- TPS ㄛ maps to TL `oo`; TPS ㄜ maps to TL `o`.
- POJ traditional version writes `o͘` as `o·` (dot variant).

## 5. Tones (聲調)

### 5.1 Tone Categories and Marks

Taiwanese has 7 tones in most dialects (plus tone 6 in Lukang/some Quanzhou-type dialects, and tone 9 for contracted syllables).

| Tone | Category | TL mark | TL numeric | POJ mark | TPS mark | Pitch value |
|------|----------|---------|------------|----------|----------|-------------|
| 1 | 陰平 (dark level) | (none) | 1 | (none) | (none) | 55 (high level) |
| 2 | 陰上 (dark rising) | ´ | 2 | ´ | ˋ | 53 (high falling) |
| 3 | 陰去 (dark departing) | ˋ | 3 | ˋ | ˪ | 11 (low level) |
| 4 | 陰入 (dark entering) | (none) | 4 | (none) | (none) | 32 (mid-falling checked) |
| 5 | 陽平 (light level) | ˆ | 5 | ˆ | ˊ | 24 (low rising) |
| 6 | (陽上) | ˇ | 6 | — | — | 33 (Quanzhou mid-level) |
| 7 | 陽去 (light departing) | ¯ | 7 | — (macron) | ˫ | 33 (mid level) |
| 8 | 陽入 (light entering) | \| | 8 | \| | ˙ | 3̩ (mid-low checked; N: high-falling checked) |
| 9 | (合音) | ˝ | 9 | ˘ | ˙ (upper) | 35 (high rising) |
| 0 | 輕聲 (neutral) | -- | 0 | -- | — | variable |

### 5.2 Tone Mark Rules

- Tones 1 and 4 have no diacritical mark in TL and POJ.
- If a syllable ends in `-p`, `-t`, `-k`, `-h`, it is entering tone (tone 4 or 8).
- Numeric tone notation: append digit after the syllable (e.g., `tong1`, `tong2`, `tok4`).

### 5.3 Tone Mark Placement (TL)

Priority order: **a > oo > e, o > i, u**

1. If `a` is present → mark on `a`. E.g.: `ài` (愛)
2. In `io` → mark on `o`. E.g.: `iô` (搖)
3. If `i` and `u` co-occur, the first is a glide and the second is the main vowel → mark on the later one. E.g.: `iû` (油), `uī` (位)
4. Standalone `m` → mark on `m`. E.g.: `m̄` (毋)
5. Digraph `oo` and nasalized `ng` → mark on the first letter. E.g.: `thôo` (塗), `n̂g` (黃)
6. Triphthong `ere` → mark on the second `e`. E.g.: `erê` (鞋)

### 5.4 Tone Sandhi (變調)

In connected speech, all syllables except the last one in a phrase undergo tone sandhi. The rules for the common 7-tone system:

| Original tone | → Sandhi tone | Example |
|---------------|---------------|---------|
| 1 (55) | → 7 (33) | 東 tong → 東風 tong(-7)-hong |
| 2 (53) | → 1 (55) | 黨 tóng → 黨員 |
| 3 (11) | → 2 (53) | 棟 tòng → 棟樑 |
| 4 (32) checked -p,-t,-k | → promoted (高促) | 督 tok → 出國 tshut-kok |
| 4 (32) checked -h glottal | → 2 (53) | 桌 toh → 隔壁 keh-piah |
| 5 (24) | → 7 (33) [泉] / 3 (11) [漳] | 同 tông → 錢財 tsînn-tsâi |
| 7 (33) | → 3 (11) | 洞 tōng → 電話 tiān-uē |
| 8 checked -p,-t,-k | → promoted (低促) | 毒 to̍k → 實力 si̍t-li̍k |
| 8 checked -h glottal | → 3 (11) | 著(燙) to̍h → 白食 pe̍h-tsia̍h |

### 5.5 Neutral Tone (輕聲)

- Written with `--` before the neutral-tone syllable in TL/POJ: e.g., `sin--ê` (新的), `tsia̍h--tsit-uánn` (食一碗)
- `--` replaces the hyphen position; no space before `--`.
- Neutral tone syllables retain their original tone mark in TL notation.
- TPS marks neutral tone at the beginning of each syllable, retaining the original tone mark.
- TLPA classifies neutral tone as tone 0.

### 5.6 Special Tone Rules

#### Tone 9 (合音)
- Tone value: 35 (high rising). TL mark: `˝`. POJ mark: `˘`.
- Occurs in contracted syllables and the first syllable of certain three-syllable phrases.
- Some dialects share the same pitch value as tone 5.
- TPS has no designated tone 9 symbol; it is provisionally represented using the TL convention.

#### Tone 6 (Lukang / Quanzhou-type)
- Only exists in certain dialects (mainly Lukang 鹿港 and some areas in western Taiwan).
- Tone value: 33 (mid level).
- POJ tone 6 mark is from Teochew tradition.
- TPS has no designated tone 6 symbol; provisionally uses `ˇ` following TL.
- In other dialects, original tone 6 words change to tone 7.

## 6. Sandhi Linked Tone Marks (主調語詞連字符)

### With base tone (本調)
人水 lâng-súi, 地動 tē-tāng, 頭疼 thâu-thiàⁿ, 水清 chúi-chheng, 雨大 hō͘-tōa

### With tone sandhi mark (~)
人水 lâng~súi, 地動 tē~tāng, 頭疼 thâu~thiàⁿ, 水清 chúi~chheng

### Distinction
- `hō͘~tōa` = general tone sandhi (all syllables before the last change)
- `~` notation is used in some educational materials to explicitly mark sandhi positions

## 8. Writing Conventions

### 8.1 Capitalization
1. Proper nouns (place names, institution names) capitalize the first letter: `Tâi-pak` (臺北), `Kàu-io̍k-pōo` (教育部)
2. Surnames and given names capitalize the first letter: `Tōo Tshong-bîng` (杜聰明)
3. First letter of a sentence is capitalized: `Guá tsin ài sńg.` (我真愛耍。)

### 8.2 Hyphen Rules (連字符)
1. Syllables within a **word** (詞) are connected by hyphens: `ha̍k-hāu` (學校)
2. Within a **phrase** (詞組), words are separated by spaces: `tsin hó` (真好)
3. Single-syllable complements and directional words connect to the preceding word: `kóng-suah` (講煞), `toh-á-tíng` (桌仔頂)

### 8.3 Neutral Tone Marker
- `--` marks the boundary between stressed and neutral-tone syllables
- The syllable before `--` reads in base tone; the syllable after `--` is neutral
- E.g.: `āu--jit` (後日), `tsáu--tshut-khì` (走出去)

### 8.4 Punctuation
- Use half-width (半形) marks for commas, periods: `, .` not `，。`
- Example: `I ài tsia̍h hî, bô ài tsia̍h bah.` (伊愛食魚，無愛食肉。)

## 11. Tone Contour Diagrams

### Common dialect (7 tones, 普遍腔)

```
5 ─── T1 ───────── T2 ══════════════════ T4(北) T8(北)
4            ╲
3 ──── T1' ───────── T7 ──── T5(漳) ──── T4(南) T8(南)
2                                  ╱
1 ───── T3 ─────── T5(泉) ────────
```

### Tone sandhi chain (普遍腔)
```
T1 → T7 → T3 → T2 → T1 (circular)
T5 → T3 (泉) / T7 (漳)
T4 (-p,-t,-k) → high checked
T4 (-h) → T2
T8 (-p,-t,-k) → low checked
T8 (-h) → T3
```

### Lukang dialect (8 tones, 鹿港腔)
Adds tone 6:
```
T1(33) → T7(33) = same pitch as T1 sandhi
T6 original → T6 sandhi varies
```
